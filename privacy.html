Privacy Policy

CutieDesk · Last updated: October 23, 2026

CutieDesk ("the App", "we", "our") is a Windows desktop application that shows clock, weather, calendar, music, notepad and countdown widgets on your desktop. This policy explains what data the App accesses, why, where it is kept, and which outside services it communicates with.

1. Summary

  • CutieDesk has no servers of its own. We do not receive, collect or store your data.
  • Everything the App keeps — sign-in tokens, notes, countdowns and settings — is stored only on your own computer.
  • The App talks directly to the services listed in section 4, and only to provide the features you use.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

2. Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect your Google account, the App uses these Google API scopes:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to list your calendars, with their names and colours, so their events can be shown together.
  • calendar.events — to show the events on those calendars, and to create, edit, repeat or delete an event when you ask it to from the Calendar widget.

Calendar data is fetched directly from Google to your computer, displayed in the widget, and kept only in memory while the App runs. It is not saved to disk, not sent to us and not shared with anyone. The App only changes your calendar when you save or delete an event yourself.

3. Spotify (optional)

If you connect your Spotify account, the App uses these Spotify scopes:

  • user-read-currently-playing and user-read-playback-state — to show the current track, artist, album art, progress, shuffle/repeat state and volume.
  • user-modify-playback-state — to play/pause, skip, shuffle, repeat and change volume when you press those controls.

This data is fetched directly from Spotify, shown in the widget, and not stored or shared.

5. What is stored on your computer

In the App's data folder (%APPDATA%\CutieDesk): Google/Spotify sign-in tokens, your notes, countdowns, widget positions and appearance, weather and clock settings, and an update log. This data never leaves your computer except as described above.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect Google or Spotify at any time in the App's Settings; this deletes that service's stored tokens.
  • Revoke the App's access from your account: Google Account permissions · Spotify connected apps.
  • Pick a weather city manually to avoid the IP-based location lookup.
  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App and removing the %APPDATA%\CutieDesk folder.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

CutieDesk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the Calendar widget's features, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and describe significant changes in the App's release notes.
