Windows desktop app

Neles Widgets

Small, pretty, draggable widgets that live on your desktop.

Neles Widgets is a lightweight Windows desktop application that displays a set of independent, freely movable widgets directly on your desktop — visible alongside your files and icons, without getting in the way of the apps you're actually using.

What it does

  • Clock — an elegant, always-visible clock and date display.
  • Weather — a multi-day local weather forecast, with a detailed view for each day.
  • Calendar — connects to your Google Calendar (optional) to show a full month view across all of your calendars, with the ability to add new events directly from the widget.
  • Now Playing — connects to your Spotify account (optional) to show what's currently playing, with album art and playback progress.
  • Notepad — a simple sticky-note widget for jotting things down, saved automatically on your own device.
  • Countdown — track the days remaining until birthdays, trips, deadlines, or any date that matters to you.

How it works

Every widget can be dragged anywhere on your screen, resized, shown or hidden individually, and recolored to match your taste — each widget has its own background color, accent color, text color, and opacity. Widgets sit at normal desktop level, so other applications you open will naturally appear in front of them, just like desktop icons.

Connecting a Google or Spotify account is entirely optional and only required for the Calendar and Now Playing widgets respectively. All of your data — sign-in tokens, notes, countdowns, and settings — is stored locally on your own computer. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what data is accessed and how it's handled.
