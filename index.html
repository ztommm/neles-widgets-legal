Privacy Policy

Neles Widgets · Last updated: July 26, 2026

Fredericia Dashboard ("the App," "we," "our") is a desktop application that displays a clock, weather, calendar, music, notes, and countdown widgets on your desktop. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it's used, and how you can control it.

1. What the App accesses, and why

  • Google Calendar — if you connect your Google account, the App reads events from your calendars and can create new events, so it can display your schedule and let you add events from the widget. This requires the calendar.readonly and calendar.events Google API scopes.
  • Spotify — if you connect your Spotify account, the App reads your current playback state (track, artist, album art, progress) so it can display what's currently playing. This requires the user-read-currently-playing and user-read-playback-state Spotify API scopes.
  • Weather — the App fetches a public weather forecast for a fixed location from Open-Meteo. This request contains no personal or account data.
  • Notes and countdowns — any text or dates you enter into the Notepad or Countdown widgets are created by you and stored only as described below.

2. Where your data is stored

The App does not operate its own data-collection servers. All data associated with your use of the App — your Google/Spotify sign-in tokens, notes, countdowns, widget positions, and appearance settings — is stored locally on your own computer, inside your operating system's application data folder for the App. We do not copy, log, or transmit this information to ourselves or to any third party.

When you sign in, your device communicates directly with Google's or Spotify's servers to authenticate you and retrieve your data; that data is then displayed locally in the App's widgets and is not routed through, or retained by, any server we operate.

3. Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

4. Your controls

  • You can disconnect Google or Spotify at any time from within the App's Settings, which deletes the locally stored sign-in tokens for that service.
  • You can revoke the App's access directly from your account at any time: Google Account permissions · Spotify connected apps.
  • You can delete all locally stored App data by removing the App's data folder from your device, or by uninstalling the App.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Neles Widgets use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
