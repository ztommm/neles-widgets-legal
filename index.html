Privacy

Your notes stay yours

CutieDesk doesn't have servers of its own, and there's no tracking or ads. Your notes, countdowns and settings live only on your computer. If you connect Google or Spotify, CutieDesk talks to them directly — nothing passes through us.

FAQ

Little questions

Anything else? Send us a message.

When can I get CutieDesk?

Very soon — we're adding the finishing touches. Want to hear when it's ready? Drop us a message.

Will the widgets get in the way of my games or apps?

No. They sit just above your wallpaper, behind whatever you're using — fullscreen games included. Show the desktop and they're right there; open something else and they step back.

Do I need a Google or Spotify account?

No, only for the Calendar and Now Playing widgets. Spotify's playback buttons (play, skip, shuffle, repeat, volume) need Spotify Premium — that's Spotify's rule, not ours.

Does it work with two monitors?

Yes. Choose which screen your widgets live on from the tray icon or Settings.

How do updates work?

CutieDesk updates itself quietly in the background — no installers to click through and nothing to re-download.
