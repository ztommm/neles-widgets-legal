Where your data goes

CutieDesk doesn't have servers, accounts or analytics. Here's everything it touches, and when.

Stays on your PC

Saved in your Windows user folder. Never uploaded.

  • Notes and countdowns
  • Widget positions, colours and settings
  • Your Google and Spotify sign-ins

Only if you connect it

CutieDesk talks to these directly. Disconnect any time in Settings.

  • Google Calendarreads your events, and changes them only when you do
  • Spotifyshows what's playing and controls playback

Public services

Used for the weather and updates. They see your IP address, like any website.

  • Open-Meteothe weather forecast
  • ipwho.isguesses your city; pick one yourself to skip this
  • GitHubchecks for app updates
Nothing comes to us. There's no CutieDesk server for it to go to. Read the full privacy policy →

Questions

Something not covered here? Send an email and you'll get a reply from the person who makes it.

When is it out?

Soon. It's in testing with a small group right now. Email us if you'd like to hear when it's ready.

Will the widgets cover my games or apps?

No. They sit just above the wallpaper, behind everything else, including fullscreen games. Show the desktop and they're there; open something and they're behind it again.

Do I need a Google or Spotify account?

Only for the Calendar and Now Playing widgets. Spotify only lets apps control playback for Premium accounts, so the play, skip, shuffle and volume buttons need Premium.

Does it work with two monitors?

Yes. Pick which screen the widgets go on from the tray icon or in Settings.

How do updates work?

The app checks for updates in the background and installs them when you close it. You don't have to download anything again.
